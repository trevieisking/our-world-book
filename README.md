# Our World

## The History We Know
### From the First Oceans to the Age of Satellites

**Status:** active book project  
**Format target:** one print-quality personal edition plus a digital/web edition  
**Source of truth:** this repository

## Core promise

> Reconstruct the history of our world as far as surviving evidence presently allows, and show the reader where evidence ends, inference begins, and uncertainty remains.

This is not a claim to possess final or absolute truth. The book separates:
- what is established by strong converging evidence;
- what is strongly supported or probable;
- what remains uncertain or actively debated;
- what is speculative, unsupported, or contradicted;
- what belongs to theology, folklore, legend, or claims that cannot presently be tested.

## Book journey

The narrative begins in the deep ocean, travels backward to the formation of Earth and the Solar System, then follows oceans, chemistry, life, evolution, extinctions, humanity, civilisation, religion, extraordinary claims, science and technology, and ends with satellites looking back at Earth from space.

## Original artwork rule

All book illustrations are to be created specifically for **Our World**. We do not plan to reproduce third-party photographs as finished book artwork.

Every generated historical or scientific scene must be labelled honestly:
- **Reconstruction** — evidence-constrained scene of an event or environment not directly photographed.
- **Scientific diagram** — explanatory schematic based on measurements or accepted models.
- **Map / timeline** — original visualisation based on cited data.
- **Artist's impression** — used only where substantial visual details are genuinely uncertain.

The artwork may be dramatic, but it must not invent uncertain details and present them as known facts.
