## Three meanings of 'hidden'

**Outside unaided human sight:** An object may be too faint, obscured, short-lived or detected at wavelengths outside ordinary vision. These possibilities do not require another spatial dimension. Rubin's six-filter range is approximately 320–1050 nanometres, spanning near-ultraviolet, visible and near-infrared light. Its combination of light collection, wide field and repeated observations expands discovery opportunities. [S17-D01, S17-D02]

**Not emitting a directly detected signal, but inferred through effects:** Gravitational lensing uses distorted images of background sources to constrain intervening gravitating mass. A reconstructed mass distribution is not a direct photograph of dark-matter particles or inhabitants of another realm. A 2026 Rubin commissioning proceeding describes weak-lensing measurements and the instrumental systematics that must be controlled. [S17-D04]

**Additional spatial dimensions or a hidden sector:** Extra dimensions are proposed additional coordinates of space; hidden sectors are proposed particles and interactions beyond the familiar ones. Some models connect these concepts, but neither requires identifying an unfamiliar wavelength as another dimension. CERN describes possible tests of extra-dimensional models, while collider collaborations test specified dark-sector signatures. These are hypotheses and searches, not observations of hidden worlds or beings. [S17-D05–D07]

## What Rubin can and cannot establish

Rubin can discover objects or changes missed by earlier observations because of sensitivity, coverage or timing. It does not outperform every telescope in every respect: Webb, for example, covers approximately 0.6–28.5 micrometres, extending much farther into infrared wavelengths. Compare instruments by the signal, wavelength, angular resolution, field and observing schedule relevant to the question, not by a universal claim that one 'sees everything'. [S17-D01–D03]

A camera needs photons in its detection range to produce a direct image. Consequently, under the assumption of no optical interaction and no ordinary-light emission reaching the instrument, increasing camera sensitivity alone cannot reveal the proposed object. An indirect gravitational or other signal would be a different measurement requiring a quantitative model. This is a conditional reasoning constraint, not proof that every hidden-sector model is impossible.

Different sensory abilities would not by themselves make an ordinary material body invisible to other observers: how it detects light and how its body emits, reflects, absorbs or scatters light are separate properties. Likewise, hypothetical occupants and craft with different visibility need a stated physical explanation. Do not use an unexplained image, an unidentified object or an instrumental artefact as confirmation of dimensional travel.

## Questions the manuscript should make explicit

What is hidden: ordinary matter outside a detector's sensitivity, a proposed new particle sector, or an additional spatial coordinate? What connects it to our measurements? What strength, range and time behaviour does that connection predict? Could ordinary sources, selection effects or instrumental errors make the same signal? What null result would exclude a specified version?

A claim about life requires additional support beyond any evidence for new particles or geometry. No discovery of a dark sector would automatically establish chemistry, organisms, civilisation, spacecraft or dimensional travel. A completely unspecified proposal with no measurable consequences is not yet a predictive scientific model.

## Addendum — extraterrestrial technology is not the same claim as extraterrestrial life

The later discussion added an important evidential distinction for Chapter 17. Even if an observation were eventually established as an engineered spacecraft, that result would not by itself show that a living organism was aboard. An engineered object could be autonomous, remotely directed, inactive or a long-lived relic. Conversely, evidence for extraterrestrial biology would not by itself establish spacecraft.

Use the following ladder whenever a disappearing or unusual object is discussed:

**authenticated signal or image -> ordinary/instrumental alternatives tested -> unresolved anomaly -> evidence of engineering -> evidence of extraterrestrial origin -> evidence concerning occupants**

Do not skip levels. An apparent disappearance from an image can result from motion, geometry, reflection, changing brightness, exposure/processing, occlusion or a transient source without requiring physical disappearance or dimensional travel.
