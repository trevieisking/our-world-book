# FUTURE RESEARCH NOTE — COSMIC TIMES, GALAXY AGES AND CIVILISATIONS

**Captured from Trev during Chapter 1 drafting. This is a hypothesis/idea ledger, not evidence.**

## Trev's observation
- We can observe and reconstruct how stars and planets form.
- Telescopes give us a kind of "fast-forward" view by showing many systems at different stages and distant systems at earlier cosmic times.
- Galaxies themselves have histories and ages.
- Planetary systems existed before Earth.
- If extraterrestrial civilisations ever exist, they need not be contemporary with humanity; some could in principle arise earlier or later.

## Scientific corrections to preserve
1. **We do not literally watch one star or planet develop over millions of years.**
   We combine snapshots of many systems with physical models; for distant objects, finite light travel time also gives genuine lookback time.

2. **A galaxy usually has a formation history, not one exact birthday.**
   Individual stellar populations, mergers and structural components can be dated with uncertainties.

3. **Planets demonstrably existed before Earth.**
   Ancient systems such as Kepler-444 establish that small planets formed billions of years before the Solar System.

4. **Earlier planets do not prove earlier life or civilisations.**
   They establish earlier opportunities/environments, not biological occupation.

5. **Civilisations before/after humanity remain possible but unconfirmed.**
   Preserve the distinction between chronological possibility and evidence.
