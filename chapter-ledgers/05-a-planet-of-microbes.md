## Evidence boundary
- Early microbial ecosystems are reconstructed from convergent sedimentary, chemical, isotopic and microscopic evidence rather than from modern-looking body fossils.
- The ca. 3.48 Ga Dresser Formation provides an especially strong ecosystem-level anchor across multiple habitats and biosignatures.
- Phototrophy is kept separate from oxygenic photosynthesis.
- Specific Dresser carbon/sulfur/nitrogen metabolic assignments remain more uncertain than the broader evidence for biological elemental cycling.
- ~3.42 Ga Barberton subseafloor filaments are treated as putative biological structures with inferred methane-linked ecology.
- ≥3.7 Ga Isua geochemical biosignatures have strengthened in 2025–2026 but retain higher uncertainty due metamorphic overprint and non-unique proxies.
- By ~3.5 Ga, an established habitat-diverse microbial biosphere is strongly supported.
