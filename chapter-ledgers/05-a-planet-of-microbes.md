## Chapter 5 final evidence boundary
- By ~3.5 Ga, an established habitat-diverse microbial biosphere is strongly supported.
- Dresser, Strelley Pool, Barberton and Isua carry different confidence levels and are not collapsed into one certainty class.
- Carbon, sulfur, iron, methane and nitrogen cycles are reconstructed at ecosystem scale while pathway-specific assignments retain uncertainty where required.
- Direct terrestrial microbial mats are established by ~3.22 Ga.
- Oxygenic photosynthesis is constrained to exist by at least ~2.87 Ga by directly dated depositional Ce oxidation; older origins remain possible and debated.
- Local oxygen oases are distinct from persistent atmospheric oxygenation.
- Chapter 6 owns the Great Oxidation Event and its planetary consequences.
