## Evidence boundary
- By ~3.5 Ga, an established, habitat-diverse microbial biosphere is strongly supported.
- Carbon, sulfur and iron cycling are reconstructed functionally where pathway-specific proxies are non-unique.
- Dresser methanogenesis remains plausible/strongly argued rather than uniquely proven.
- Biological nitrogen fixation is anchored securely by ~3.2 Ga; older nitrogen burial is not promoted into pathway-specific proof.
- Syntrophy and mat zonation are used as ecological mechanisms/analogues, not as directly fossilised named partnerships.

## Next target
