# Chapter 4 support register — pages 137–144

This register is authoritative for pages 137–144 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **137 — protometabolism:** metabolism is treated functionally as sustained, connected matter/energy transformation rather than as a requirement for modern ATP-, enzyme- and gene-controlled pathways. Chemical disequilibrium supplies free energy but is not itself metabolism. **Status: ESTABLISHED thermodynamic framing / historical implementation UNKNOWN.**
- **138 — redox and geological H₂:** serpentinization is an established water–rock process capable of generating H₂ and alkaline reducing fluids. 2025 iron-sulfide chemical-garden experiments generated abiotic H₂ sufficient to fuel a modern hydrogenotrophic archaeon. **Status: ESTABLISHED energy source / origin role HYPOTHETICAL.**
- **139 — alkaline-vent gradients:** alkaline hydrothermal systems can generate strong geochemical disequilibria and are a major origin-of-life candidate environment. The claim that thin inorganic barriers maintained useful natural proton gradients that prebiotic chemistry could harness remains contested; 2016 critiques identify unresolved membrane/energy-coupling problems. **Status: PLAUSIBLE / CONTESTED.**
- **140 — mineral catalysis:** Fe, Ni, Co, iron sulfides and other minerals can catalyse origin-relevant redox and carbon chemistry. Ancient Fe–S cofactors in biology make mineral continuity hypotheses attractive, but chemical/structural similarity does not prove direct ancestry. **Status: ESTABLISHED catalysis / ancestry hypothesis UNRESOLVED.**
- **141 — geochemical CO₂ fixation:** native Fe⁰, Ni⁰ and Co⁰ can reduce CO₂ in water to acetate and pyruvate under hydrothermal-like laboratory conditions, producing intermediates/end-products overlapping the acetyl-CoA pathway. **Status: ESTABLISHED non-enzymatic reaction / complete ancestral pathway UNPROVEN.**
- **142 — protometabolic networks:** metal-driven experiments have reproduced 6/11 reverse-TCA reactions, connected three-step sequences, Fe²⁺ networks producing 9/11 TCA intermediates, and H₂/Ni-driven metabolism-like amino-acid chemistry under shared conditions. **Status: ESTABLISHED partial/connected networks / autonomous cycle UNDEMONSTRATED.**
- **143 — cycle boundary:** a true sustained cycle requires all necessary reactions, intermediate regeneration, compatible catalysts/conditions, selectivity and resistance to side-reaction drain. The classic Orgel critique remains relevant even as later work strengthens partial-network plausibility. **Status: ESTABLISHED systems constraint.**
- **144 — RNA/metabolism false binary:** RNA replication depends on energy and feedstock chemistry; protometabolism without heredity cannot support open-ended Darwinian evolution. Hybrid/co-emergence frameworks are scientifically viable, but no unique chronological ordering is established. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED integration problem / historical sequence UNCERTAIN.**
