## Scope and evidence boundary

This section follows named fossils rather than an invented universal Cambrian community. Chengjiang, the Burgess Shale and early Cambrian reef records remain distinct in place and time. A relationship on a phylogenetic tree does not identify a fossil as a direct ancestor.

The 2025 reinterpretation of *Shishania* supersedes an unqualified use of its 2024 molluscan assignment. Museum records are useful for specimen anatomy and locality but older taxonomy, ages and ecological labels are checked against later research. Anatomical observations, reconstructed functions and inferred ecological benefits receive separate grades.
