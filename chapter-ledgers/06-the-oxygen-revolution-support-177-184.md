## Evidence checkpoints
- **177 — GOE definition:** the GOE is the interval when atmospheric O2 first accumulated persistently; it is not the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis, the first O2 molecule, or immediate whole-ocean oxygenation. Current 2026 syntheses treat its onset as broadly ~2.5–2.4 Ga with a longer Paleoproterozoic transition. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad transition / detailed trajectory ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **178 — sulfur MIF:** atmospheric UV photochemistry under extremely low O2 produces/preserves mass-independent sulfur isotope fractionation. Its disappearance is a major global atmospheric proxy; threshold estimates near ~10^-6 PAL are model-dependent, and crustal-memory recycling complicates youngest-signal dating. **Status: ESTABLISHED proxy / threshold details model-dependent.**
- **179 — chronology and persistence:** regional S-MIF records can be asynchronous. 2025 multi-proxy work places a major transition near ~2.32 Ga toward persistent shallow-ocean oxygenation, while deep waters remain largely anoxic. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED threshold sequence / exact basin-to-global chronology debated.**
- **180 — oxygen budget:** long-term O2 accumulation requires oxidant sources to exceed reducing sinks. Organic-carbon/pyrite burial, productivity, weathering, degassing, hydrogen escape and tectonic fluxes all enter the budget. **Status: ESTABLISHED source–sink framework / dominant trigger UNRESOLVED.**
- **181 — carbon burial and nutrients:** organic-carbon burial preserves oxidising power; 2025 phosphate/carbon-isotope work supports marine phosphorus availability as a contributor to productivity and O2 fluctuations during the GOE, especially later phases. **Status: ESTABLISHED mechanism / contribution magnitude debated.**
- **182 — solid-Earth sinks:** reduced volcanic/metamorphic gases, hydrothermal reductants and fresh crust consume O2; tectonic/continental evolution can modify both sinks and nutrient supply. **Status: ESTABLISHED processes / integrated causal balance unresolved.**
- **183 — climate coupling:** methane oxidation provides a plausible link between rising O2 and Paleoproterozoic cooling/glaciation, but CO2, weathering, haze, tectonics and other feedbacks preclude a single-cause claim. Ozone formation begins as O2 rises. **Status: PLAUSIBLE contribution / glaciation causality multi-factor.**
- **184 — geological/ecological response:** oxidative weathering removes reduced detrital minerals, expands sulfate/trace-metal mobility and promotes shallow-ocean oxygenation while much of the deeper ocean remains ferruginous or euxinic. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad consequences.**
