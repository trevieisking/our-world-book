# Chapter 7 support register — pages 217–224

This register is authoritative for pages 217–224 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **217 — earliest convincing eukaryotic fossils:** late Palaeoproterozoic–Mesoproterozoic eukaryotic microfossils are identified through morphology plus sedimentary/redox context; 2026 work associates the oldest known fossil eukaryotes (~1.75–1.4 Ga) primarily with oxygenated benthic settings. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
- **218 — molecular fossils:** protosteroids reveal an ecologically important deep-branching steroid-producing biota from at least ~1.64 to ~0.8 Ga. Molecular fossils require stringent syngeneity/contamination controls and do not uniquely identify individual taxa. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED biochemical/ecological signal / producer identity partly uncertain.**
- **219 — repeated multicellularity:** multicellularity evolved independently many times; at least 45 independent eukaryotic origins have been identified under explicit criteria. *Bangiomorpha* (~1.047 Ga) provides a strong crown-red-algal multicellular calibration; older forms such as *Qingshania* remain less secure in exact affinity. **Status: ESTABLISHED convergence / fossil-specific affinities vary.**
- **220 — organismal cooperation:** clonal and aggregative routes both generate multicellular systems. A 2026 unicellular animal-relative study demonstrates aggregative multicellularity and differentiated states, constraining possible pre-animal capabilities without identifying an ancestor. **Status: ESTABLISHED modern constraint / ancestral route unresolved.**
- **221 — Snowball Earth:** Sturtian (~717–659 Ma) and Marinoan (~645–635 Ma) glaciations were exceptionally severe and reached low latitudes. Full hard-Snowball ocean sealing versus thinner/open-water variants remains debated. **Status: ESTABLISHED extreme global glaciation / detailed ice state ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **222 — survival/refugia and deglaciation:** modern McMurdo supraglacial ponds demonstrate that ice-dominated settings can support diverse eukaryotes and are plausible refugia analogues, not direct Cryogenian evidence. 2025 post-Sturtian geochemistry indicates deoxygenation followed by still largely anoxic, nutrient-limited conditions rather than automatic postglacial oxygenation. **Status: PLAUSIBLE REFUGIA / STRONGLY SUPPORTED caution against simple oxygen trigger.**
- **223 — Ediacaran communities:** earliest widespread macroscopic animal communities occur by ~574 Ma. Rangeomorphs are increasingly interpreted as early total-group/stem eumetazoans, while individual Ediacaran taxa retain different affinity confidence. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED animal ecosystems / taxon-specific uncertainty.**
- **224 — Cambrian threshold:** animals predate the Cambrian; the Cambrian radiation reflects genuine ecological/developmental diversification plus improved fossil visibility through skeletons and exceptional preservation. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad framing / causal weighting unresolved.**
