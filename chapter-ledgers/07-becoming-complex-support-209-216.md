## Evidence checkpoints
- **209 — nucleus:** LECA had a nucleus/nuclear pores; information-processing systems show strong Asgard ancestry, but the membrane-bounded origin of the nucleus and membrane-replacement sequence remain unresolved. **Status: ESTABLISHED LECA feature / origin mechanism UNCERTAIN.**
- **210 — cytoskeleton/endomembranes:** Asgard homologues provide precursors, and 2025–2026 work supports extensive gene duplication and substantial host complexity before LECA. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED precursors / event ordering model-dependent.**
- **211 — phagocytosis:** no cultured Asgard is demonstrated to perform modern phagocytosis. 2025 duplication dating supports endocytic/phagocytosis-related machinery before or around mitochondrial acquisition but cannot resolve exact ordering. **Status: ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **212 — chromosome control:** comparative reconstruction gives LECA linear chromosomes, centromeres/telomeres, nucleosomal chromatin, mitosis and complex segregation machinery. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
- **213 — spliceosome/introns:** LECA was intron-rich with a sophisticated spliceosome. Group II intron ancestry is strongly supported; mitochondrial delivery and nucleus/intron causal ordering remain hypotheses. **Status: ESTABLISHED LECA state / origin sequence UNCERTAIN.**
- **214 — sexual LECA:** meiosis-specific machinery across deep eukaryotic lineages strongly supports sexual capacity in LECA; sexes/gamete dimorphism are not projected backward. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
- **215 — why sex persists:** repair, purging deleterious mutations, combining beneficial alleles, parasite/environmental change and organelle-genome conflict all provide mechanisms; no universal single explanation is accepted. **Status: MULTIPLE SUPPORTED MECHANISMS / original driver UNKNOWN.**
- **216 — assembled complex cell:** LECA was a highly integrated unicellular eukaryote; exact dates and internal event order remain uncertain. **Status: ESTABLISHED endpoint / chronology ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
