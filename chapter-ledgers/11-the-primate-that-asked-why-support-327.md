## Sources and access
- **S11-78:** Granger, D.E. et al. (2015), *New cosmogenic burial ages for Sterkfontein Member 2 Australopithecus and Member 5 Oldowan*. Nature 522, 85–88. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14268 ; https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14268 . Publisher article/editorial summary checked 2026-10-04. StW 573-bearing Member 2 is dated to ~3.67 Ma.
- **S11-79:** Granger, D.E. et al. (2022), *Cosmogenic nuclide dating of Australopithecus at Sterkfontein, South Africa*. PNAS 119, e2123516119. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2123516119 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9271183/ . Open full text checked 2026-10-04. Reports a 3.41 ± 0.11 Ma Member 4 isochron and argues younger flowstones are intrusive.
- **S11-80:** Clarke, R.J. & Kuman, K. / related StW 573 anatomical series, including Clarke et al. Used to document the published *A. prometheus* attribution, not to declare it settled.
- **S11-81:** Martin, J.M. et al. (2025), *The StW 573 Little Foot Fossil Should Not Be Attributed to Australopithecus prometheus*. American Journal of Biological Anthropology 188, e70177. https://doi.org/10.1002/ajpa.70177 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41316916/ . Open/indexed abstract checked 2026-10-04. Rejects StW 573 assignment to *A. prometheus* and argues the name remains a junior synonym of *A. africanus*, while noting Little Foot's distinctiveness.
- **S11-82:** Thackeray, J.F. (2024), *A biochronological date of 3.6 million years for “Little Foot” (StW 573, Australopithecus prometheus from Sterkfontein, South Africa)*. Reviews the continuing chronology debate and produces a ~3.6 Ma model estimate compatible with cosmogenic Member 2 dating.
