# Chapter 2 support register — pages 081–084

This compact register is authoritative for pages 081–084 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **081 — bombardment history:** intense early bombardment is established, but one narrow 3.9-Ga terminal cataclysm is not treated as settled. Best sources: Bottke & Norman (2017); Koeberl, Schulz & Huber (2024).
- **082 — impact consequences:** large impacts can melt/vaporise crust, remove or add atmosphere, heat oceans and generate long-lived hydrothermal systems. Thermal models do not require the entire Hadean crust to remain globally molten at once. Best sources: Abramov, Kring & Mojzsis (2013); Abramov & Mojzsis (2009).
- **083 — surviving record:** Hadean zircons survived incomplete thermal resetting; direct terrestrial impact-spherule layers are preserved by about 3.47 Ga even where their craters are gone. Best sources: Abbott et al. (2012); Simonson & Glass (2004); Koeberl et al. (2024).
- **084 — transition:** a hydrosphere may have existed by ~4.4 Ga but was episodically vaporised; robust direct subaqueous rock evidence is younger, around ~3.7 Ga. Best source: Vulpius et al. (2026).
