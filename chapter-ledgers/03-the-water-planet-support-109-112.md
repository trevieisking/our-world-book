# Chapter 3 support register — pages 109–112

This register is authoritative for pages 109–112 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **109 — extraterrestrial organics:** carbonaceous meteorites contain indigenous amino acids, polyols and other organics. This demonstrates abiotic extraterrestrial organic chemistry, not extraterrestrial life.
- **110 — endogenous organics:** laboratory and experimental work establishes multiple abiotic routes to organic molecules through electrical discharge, UV, impacts, water–rock reactions and hydrothermal chemistry. Individual reactions do not reconstruct one full historical sequence.
- **111 — molecules versus life:** replication, energy coupling/metabolism, compartmentalisation and heredity/evolution must be integrated into a sustained system; current experiments demonstrate important subsystems but not a complete autonomous prebiotic cell.
- **112 — evidence boundary:** the origin of life remains unresolved. No single experiment currently demonstrates the complete natural transition from plausible early-Earth geochemistry to an autonomous system capable of open-ended Darwinian evolution.
