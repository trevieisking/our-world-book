## Sources and access
- **S11-103:** Herries, A.I.R. et al. (2020), *Contemporaneity of Australopithecus, Paranthropus, and early Homo erectus in South Africa*. Science 368, eaaw7293. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaw7293 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32241925/ . Indexed abstract checked 2026-10-04. DNH 134 is dated within ~2.04–1.95 Ma and described as a cranium with clear *H. erectus* affinities.
- **S11-104:** Hammond, A.S. et al. (2021), *New hominin remains and revised context from the earliest Homo erectus locality in East Turkana, Kenya*. Nature Communications 12. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-22208-x ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8044126/ . Open full text checked 2026-10-04. Relocates KNM-ER 2598 context and supports age >1.85 Ma, consistent with ~1.88–1.9 Ma placement.
- **S11-103:** Crompton, R.H. et al. (2004), *The role of load-carrying in the evolution of modern body proportions*. Journal of Anatomy 204, 417–430. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1571301/ . Open article checked 2026-10-04. Models Nariokotome's longer-legged proportions and locomotor/load-carrying mechanics.
- **S11-104:** García-Martínez, D. et al. (2020), *Rib cage anatomy in Homo erectus suggests a recent evolutionary origin of modern human body shape*. Nature Ecology & Evolution 4, 1178–1187. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-020-1240-4 ; https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-020-1240-4 . Publisher article/abstract checked 2026-10-04. 3D reconstruction finds a short, wide and deep thorax unlike modern humans.
- **S11-103:** Cunningham, D.L. et al. (2018), *The effect of ontogeny on estimates of KNM-WT 15000's adult body size*. Journal of Human Evolution 121, 119–127. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhevol.2018.04.002 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29754743/ . Indexed abstract checked 2026-10-04. Demonstrates that projected adult size varies materially with growth-model assumptions.
