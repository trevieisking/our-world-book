# Chapter 3 support register — pages 085–092

This register is authoritative for pages 085–092 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **085:** early liquid water is supported by Hadean zircon geochemistry, while exact ocean extent, depth, salinity and geography remain unknown.
- **086:** ~3.8–3.7 Ga Isua pillow lavas and associated sedimentary rocks provide direct evidence of subaqueous environments.
- **087:** Hadean/Archean seawater was chemically unlike modern seawater; anoxic conditions, stronger water–rock interaction, high dissolved silica and different Fe/Mg/Ca/S chemistry are strongly supported, while exact salinity and pH remain uncertain.
- **088:** the young Sun was substantially fainter; liquid water therefore requires a climate explanation involving greenhouse gases, albedo and feedbacks rather than one single settled mechanism.
- **089:** atmosphere–ocean–rock carbon exchange created long-term climate feedbacks, but the early strengths of continental versus seafloor weathering are uncertain.
- **090:** water–rock interaction and hydrothermal circulation strongly altered ocean chemistry; modern ridge geometry is not projected uncritically onto the Hadean.
- **091:** meteoric-water evidence and early Archean detrital sediments support exposed crust and active weathering, but no global land fraction is assigned.
- **092:** early-ocean temperature reconstructions remain controversial because oxygen-isotope signals depend on both temperature and seawater composition and may be altered later.
