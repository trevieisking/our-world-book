## Evidence checkpoints
- **193 — oxygen toxicity:** O2 exposure generates reactive oxygen species that damage DNA, proteins, lipids and metal centres. Antioxidant/repair systems are ancient but their individual origins are difficult to date. **Status: ESTABLISHED chemistry / evolutionary chronology UNCERTAIN.**
- **194 — aerobic respiration:** O2 is a high-potential terminal electron acceptor and can greatly increase usable energy yield from many substrates. Pre-GOE aerobic respiration is plausible in local oxygen oases, while 2026 molecular work places major terminal oxidase expansion after the GOE. **Status: ESTABLISHED energetic advantage / origin timing ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **195 — ecological restructuring:** persistent O2 creates oxic, microoxic and anoxic niches; anaerobic metabolisms persist in reducing habitats. **Status: ESTABLISHED ecological consequence.**
- **196 — ozone:** atmospheric O2 enables O3 formation and progressive UV shielding; exact ozone columns/surface UV during the GOE are model-dependent. **Status: ESTABLISHED process / quantitative history uncertain.**
- **197 — Lomagundi–Jatuli:** ~2.22–2.06 Ga positive carbonate carbon-isotope excursion records extraordinary carbon-cycle/redox reorganisation. Enhanced organic burial and higher O2 are plausible, but absolute O2 magnitude and global interpretation remain debated. **Status: ESTABLISHED excursion / overshoot amplitude ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **198 — post-Lomagundi oxygen:** multiple proxies indicate relative deoxygenation and return of widespread ocean anoxia, while the atmosphere remains fundamentally more oxidised than the Archean. Exact timing/global extent of decline remain debated. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED relative decline / exact trajectory unresolved.**
- **199 — oxygen versus complexity:** persistent Paleoproterozoic O2 does not map simply onto eukaryogenesis or immediate high-O2-demand complexity. Oxygen is an environmental enabler/constraint, not a sufficient cause. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
- **200 — chapter boundary:** the GOE permanently changes Earth's atmospheric baseline and evolutionary possibility space without creating a modern ocean or automatically generating eukaryotes. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad sequence.**
