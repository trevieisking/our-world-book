## Chapter 4 status
**Pages 113–120 are TEXT LOCKED for Phase A.**

The opening section establishes the operational boundary between chemistry and biology and separates:
- a working definition of life from a universal definition;
- replication from heritable Darwinian evolution;
- modern-cell machinery from primitive functional requirements;
- a biosignature's biogenicity from its syngeneity;
- “life existed by” from “life originated at”.

## Current evidence boundary
- ca. **3.48 Ga Dresser Formation:** conservative convincing early-life anchor.
- **>3.7 Ga Isua:** substantial and strengthening older evidence, but preserved in a more altered record.
- **Nuvvuagittuq:** potentially much older claim, still unresolved at the biosignature/host-age interface.
- **LUCA:** not first life; molecular-clock ages are indirect and model-dependent.
