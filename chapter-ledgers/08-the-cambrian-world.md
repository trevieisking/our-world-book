## Evidence boundaries

- Animals predate the formal Cambrian boundary.
- The reference point and its numerical age are distinct.
- First fossil appearance is not necessarily lineage origination.
- Burrowing, skeletons, sensory systems and food webs are interacting changes, with different records and uncertainties.
- The *Lapworthella* prey record is stronger than the inferred reciprocal predator response.
- Optical and mechanical anatomy constrain ecological hypotheses without reproducing ancient experience.
- Exceptional fossil deposits are site-specific, filtered archives.
- Stem/crown definitions do not establish a fossil as a direct ancestor.
- *Hallucigenia* placement remains disputed; *Shishania* includes the 2025 reinterpretation; *Pikaia* uses the revised 2024 orientation.
- Gut contents, attachment and modelled flow have different evidential strengths from capture, host benefit and pumping behaviour.
- Chengjiang, Burgess Shale and early reef communities remain separate in place and time.
