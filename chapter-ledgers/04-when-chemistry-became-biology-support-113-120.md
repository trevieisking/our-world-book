# Chapter 4 support register — pages 113–120

This register is authoritative for pages 113–120 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **113 — operational definition of life:** there is no universally binding scientific definition of life. A widely used astrobiology working definition emphasises a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution. **Status: ESTABLISHED that definitions vary; working definition is operational, not official law.**
- **114 — heredity and selection:** Darwinian evolution requires multiplication, heritable variation and differential persistence/reproduction. Copying alone is insufficient. **Status: ESTABLISHED evolutionary principle.**
- **115 — integrated living chemistry:** origin models must ultimately connect replication/heredity with energy coupling, catalysis and some form of confinement/compartmentalisation in a non-equilibrium system. Modern lipid membranes and protein metabolism are not assumed to be primitive prerequisites. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED functional framework; historical implementation unresolved.**
- **116 — biosignature method:** biogenicity and syngeneity must both be tested. Morphology, isotopes or organic carbon alone can produce false positives; convergent geological, chemical and textural evidence is stronger. **Status: ESTABLISHED methodology.**
- **117 — conservative early-life anchor:** the ca. 3.48 Ga Dresser Formation preserves multiple mutually reinforcing biosignatures and is widely treated as among Earth's earliest convincing records of life. **Status: ESTABLISHED / STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
- **118 — Isua >3.7 Ga:** proposed stromatolite morphologies were convincingly challenged as deformation structures, but independent carbonaceous and isotope evidence from metasediments has strengthened, including 2025–2026 work distinguishing sedimentary graphite from vein graphite and suggesting anaerobic metabolisms. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED older biosphere interpretation / ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **119 — Nuvvuagittuq older claims:** putative hydrothermal microfossils were proposed at >3.77 Ga and potentially much older; 2025 geochronology strengthens the Hadean age of parts of the belt, while the age and syngeneity of the specific fossil-bearing material and its biogenicity remain disputed. **Status: UNCERTAIN.**
- **120 — existence date versus origin date:** geological biosignatures establish that life existed by a date, not that abiogenesis occurred at that date. LUCA is not first life. A 2024 molecular-clock analysis places LUCA near 4.2 Ga but this is an indirect, model-dependent estimate. **Status: ESTABLISHED conceptual distinction / MODEL-DEPENDENT chronology.**
