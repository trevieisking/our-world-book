## Chapter 6 final evidence boundary
- The GOE is a protracted/possibly oscillatory atmospheric/surface redox transition, not the origin of oxygenic photosynthesis.
- S-MIF, red beds, reduced detrital minerals, trace metals and other proxies constrain different thresholds and environments.
- Persistent atmospheric O2 predates broad deep-ocean oxygenation; ferruginous/euxinic anoxia remains extensive.
- O2 accumulation reflects a source–sink budget involving productivity, burial, reductant fluxes, weathering, tectonics and hydrogen escape; no single trigger is selected.
- Lomagundi records extraordinary carbon-cycle/redox reorganisation and probable enhanced oxygenation, but the magnitude of any O2 overshoot remains debated.
- Oxygen is both toxin and energetic opportunity; its rise restructures ecology, UV shielding, nutrients and mineral diversity.
- Persistent oxygen expands evolutionary possibility but is not treated as a sufficient cause of eukaryogenesis.
