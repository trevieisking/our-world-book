# Chapter 5 support register — pages 153–160

This register is authoritative for pages 153–160 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **153 — microbial-scale biosphere:** early-life reconstruction relies mainly on community structures, organic matter, minerals and isotope patterns rather than large body fossils. By ca. 3.48 Ga, convergent Palaeoarchaean evidence supports an established microbial biosphere. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad conclusion.**
- **154 — mats, MISS and stromatolites:** biofilms/microbial mats can alter sediment and generate MISS/stromatolites, but abiotic sedimentation, mineral precipitation and deformation can mimic some shapes. Geological context and repeated associations are required. **Status: ESTABLISHED methodology.**
- **155 — Dresser landscape:** the ca. 3.48 Ga Dresser Formation records a heterogeneous volcanic–hydrothermal caldera influenced by marine, hydrothermal and subaerial fluids. Stromatolites, MISS and hot-spring-associated biosignatures occur in this dynamic setting. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED environmental reconstruction.**
- **156 — phototrophy before oxygen:** shallow illuminated Palaeoarchaean mats are consistent with phototrophy. Such evidence does not establish oxygenic photosynthesis or cyanobacteria; anoxygenic phototrophs provide viable analogues. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED phototrophy / oxygenic mechanism UNESTABLISHED.**
- **157 — Dresser elemental cycling:** carbon, sulfur and nitrogen isotope data support biological element cycling, but specific assignments such as methanogenesis, sulfur disproportionation/sulfate reduction and N fixation depend on non-unique proxies and geological context. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED broad cycling / metabolism-specific interpretations vary.**
- **158 — Barberton subsurface life:** ~3.42 Ga filamentous structures in a palaeo-subseafloor hydrothermal vein system are interpreted as putative indigenous microorganisms with possible methane-linked metabolism. **Status: STRONG CANDIDATE / taxonomic-metabolic assignment INFERENTIAL.**
- **159 — Isua >3.7 Ga:** 2025 work distinguishes sediment-associated graphite interpreted as detrital biogenic organic matter from more variable vein graphite; 2026 carbon/sulfur isotope work is consistent with multiple anaerobic respiratory metabolisms. Heavy metamorphic overprint and abiotic alternatives require higher uncertainty. **Status: SUBSTANTIAL AND STRENGTHENING / ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **160 — established Palaeoarchaean biosphere:** combined evidence supports habitat and metabolic diversification by ~3.5 Ga, including surface mats and probable subsurface communities. Exact lineages and many pathway assignments remain unknown. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED ecosystem-level conclusion.**
