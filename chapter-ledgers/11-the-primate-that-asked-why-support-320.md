## Sources and access
- **S11-53:** Ward, C.V., Leakey, M.G. & Walker, A. (2001), *Morphology of Australopithecus anamensis from Kanapoi and Allia Bay, Kenya*. Journal of Human Evolution 41, 255–368. https://doi.org/10.1006/jhev.2001.0507 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11599925/ . Bibliographic record, indexed material and article-access summary checked 2026-10-04. The Kanapoi tibial orientation and joint morphology are used as evidence for habitual bipedality; no complete modern-human gait is inferred.
- **S11-54:** Leakey, M.G., Feibel, C.S., McDougall, I., Ward, C.S. & Walker, A. (1998), *New specimens and confirmation of an early age for Australopithecus anamensis*. Nature 393, 62–66. https://doi.org/10.1038/29972 ; https://www.nature.com/articles/29972 . Publisher abstract checked 2026-10-04. It confirms early Kanapoi material between tephra dated around 4.17–4.12 Ma and discusses the tibia/humerus/mandible sample.
- **S11-55:** Haile-Selassie, Y. et al. (2019), *A 3.8-million-year-old hominin cranium from Woranso-Mille, Ethiopia*. Nature 573, 214–219. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1513-8 ; https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1513-8 . Publisher article/abstract checked 2026-10-04. MRD is assigned to *A. anamensis* from canine, maxillary and temporal morphology; authors infer at least 100 kyr overlap with *A. afarensis* and reject the formerly simple anagenesis model.
- **S11-56:** Saylor, B.Z. et al. (2019), *Age and context of mid-Pliocene hominin cranium from Woranso-Mille, Ethiopia*. Nature 573, 220–224. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1514-7 ; https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1514-7 . Publisher abstract checked 2026-10-04. Volcanic correlation, palaeomagnetism and Bayesian age modelling constrain the deltaic fossil-bearing strata to approximately 3.804–3.777 Ma; environmental reconstruction is not overextended into the locomotor claim.

## Checks
The manuscript does not treat *Australopithecus* as a single straight ancestor ladder. Bipedality is supported anatomically; MRD's assignment and age are separately sourced; temporal overlap is used to reject only the simplest no-overlap anagenesis scenario.
