## Evidence checkpoints
- **145 — no single origin flask:** successful origin-of-life chemistries often require diverse physical/chemical conditions. Connected multi-environment scenarios are physically plausible but must satisfy transport, survival, compatibility and concentration constraints. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED systems issue / historical route UNKNOWN.**
- **146 — wet–dry cycling:** hydration/dehydration cycles concentrate solutes, can promote condensation/polymerisation and can reorganise lipid/polymer mixtures into protocell-like compartments on rehydration. Terrestrial hot-spring scenarios remain hypotheses and depend on suitable exposed land/freshwater settings. **Status: ESTABLISHED component mechanisms / birthplace UNPROVEN.**
- **147 — ultraviolet photochemistry:** UV-driven cyanosulfidic chemistry can generate precursors relevant to RNA, amino acids and lipids from related feedstocks. UV can also degrade organics, so dose, shielding, timing and transport matter. **Status: ESTABLISHED laboratory chemistry / environmental integration UNRESOLVED.**
- **148 — eutectic ice:** freezing excludes solutes into concentrated liquid channels, can protect/concentrate RNA and support RNA polymerase-ribozyme activity. 2025 pH/freeze–thaw cycling enabled open-ended RNA replication/selection in a laboratory system. **Status: ESTABLISHED physical/chemical mechanism / historical role UNKNOWN.**
- **149 — environment trade-offs:** vents, surface pools, UV-lit settings, ice and mineral surfaces each solve different concentration/energy/catalysis problems while creating others. **Status: ESTABLISHED comparative constraint; no birthplace ranking assigned.**
- **150 — planet as apparatus:** natural transport/cycling can in principle replace some laboratory interventions. Multi-stage origin scenarios require explicit physical handoffs between settings; examples exist for mineral-to-vesicle encapsulation, wet–dry transfer and freeze–thaw cycling, but no complete historical sequence is demonstrated. **Status: PLAUSIBLE framework / UNPROVEN history.**
- **151 — 2026 experimental inventory:** many required subsystems now have experimentally demonstrated component mechanisms: organic synthesis, nucleotide chemistry, catalytic RNA, RNA replication/selection, primitive membranes, protocell growth/division/fitness coupling, geochemical carbon reduction and protometabolic networks. Modern synthetic cells integrate more functions but rely on evolved biological machinery. **Status: ESTABLISHED component advances.**
- **152 — final boundary:** no complete autonomous transition from plausible simple geochemical feedstocks to a self-maintaining cellular system capable of indefinite open-ended Darwinian evolution has been demonstrated; abiogenesis mechanism, location and date remain unresolved. Geological evidence nevertheless establishes a very early biosphere. **Status: ESTABLISHED evidence boundary.**

## Chapter 4 final evidence boundary
The origin of life remains an unresolved historical and mechanistic problem. Many necessary component processes are experimentally demonstrated under plausible or partially plausible conditions, but no single experiment or linked experimental sequence currently demonstrates the complete autonomous natural transition from simple early-Earth geochemistry to an indefinitely self-maintaining, open-ended Darwinian cellular lineage.
