# Chapter 5 support register — pages 169–176

This register is authoritative for pages 169–176 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **169 — Strelley Pool stromatolite diversification:** ~3.4 Ga Strelley Pool stromatolites occur across a sediment-starved peritidal carbonate platform and vary with water depth, sediment influx and hydrothermal influence. Some individual conical structures retain abiotic-sinter interpretations. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED ecosystem record / individual structures vary.**
- **170 — microfossil criteria:** Strelley Pool carbonaceous spheroids, lenticular forms, filaments and films are evaluated through depositional context, carbon chemistry, population structure, wall organisation and nanoscale comparison with abiotic biomorphs. **Status: COMPELLING cellular evidence for portions of the assemblage / taxonomic identity UNKNOWN.**
- **171 — Apex Chert warning:** ~3.46 Ga Apex filaments remain contested between biogenic and hydrothermal/mineral-artefact interpretations. Their scientific importance is methodological: 3D morphology, primary context, thermal history and false-positive testing are essential. **Status: CONTESTED.**
- **172 — terrestrial colonisation:** 3.22 Ga Moodies Group fluvial microbial mats provide direct fossil evidence of life on land. Nitrogen-cycle interpretations are secondary to the stronger terrestrial-biogenic conclusion. **Status: ESTABLISHED local terrestrial life.**
- **173 — oxygenic photosynthesis minimum age:** Patry et al. (2025) directly dated depositional Ce oxidation in 2.87, 2.85 and 2.78 Ga carbonate platforms, requiring O2 production and placing oxygenic photosynthesis in the Mesoarchaean or earlier. **Status: STRONG DIRECTLY DATED MINIMUM CONSTRAINT; origin date older/UNKNOWN.**
- **174 — oxygen oases:** local/episodic oxygen accumulation predates global oxygenation. 2025 Tl-isotope work indicates transient marine bottom-water oxygenation by ~2.65 Ga, and a 2025 synthesis supports oxygenated Neoarchaean shelf waters generated by benthic mats. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED late-Archean local oxygenation.**
- **175 — delayed oxygenation:** oxygen production could precede atmospheric accumulation because Fe2+, reduced sulfur, volcanic/hydrothermal gases, reduced crust, methane and organic re-oxidation consume O2. 2025 modelling shows nutrient limitation and ocean redox structure can extend the delay; multiple controls remain viable. **Status: ESTABLISHED sink principle / exact dominant controls UNRESOLVED.**
- **176 — chapter boundary:** microbial ecology and biogeochemical cycling were established long before animals/plants. Oxygenic photosynthesis predates the global atmospheric transition; Chapter 6 begins when planetary sinks no longer prevent persistent surface oxygenation. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad sequence.**
