# Chapter 6 support register — pages 185–192

This register is authoritative for pages 185–192 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **185 — detrital mineral loss:** Archean/earliest Paleoproterozoic detrital pyrite and uraninite indicate very low-O2 surface transport; their disappearance accompanies oxidative weathering. Source/recycling/transport context is required. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad proxy.**
- **186 — red beds:** red colour alone is not diagnostic. 2024 Huronian work identifies early hematite coatings in ~2.31 Ga terrestrial fluvial sandstones, providing direct evidence for widespread terrestrial Fe oxidation near the GOE. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED dated terrestrial proxy.**
- **187 — BIFs:** banded iron formations predate and peak before/around the GOE. Fe2+ oxidation can be biological or abiotic; 2025 nitrogen-isotope data record a non-monotonic seawater oxygenation history in Hamersley BIF environments. **Status: ESTABLISHED multi-process redox archive / no single universal oxidation mechanism.**
- **188 — sulfate expansion:** oxidative pyrite weathering expands sulfate delivery, supporting more microbial sulfate reduction and sulfide production; pyrite burial affects long-term redox balance. **Status: ESTABLISHED process / concentration history model-dependent.**
- **189 — stratified ocean:** persistent shallow oxygenation coexisted with ferruginous and euxinic deeper waters; atmosphere and ocean oxygenation are not synchronous at all scales. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED.**
- **190 — nutrient/metal redistribution:** O2, Fe oxides and sulfide alter Fe, Mo, P, Ni, Cu and other element availability, creating ecological feedbacks and nutrient limits. **Status: ESTABLISHED redox chemistry / biological consequences context-dependent.**
- **191 — mineral diversification:** biologically driven oxygenation expanded near-surface high-valence mineral diversity. Large mineral databases support major redox-sensitive mineral changes; quantitative atmospheric reconstructions from mineral inventories retain preservation/sampling caveats. **Status: ESTABLISHED qualitative diversification / quantitative inference ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **192 — post-GOE heterogeneity:** persistent atmospheric O2 did not immediately oxygenate the deep ocean; anoxic microbial niches remained extensive. **Status: ESTABLISHED broad Proterozoic condition.**
