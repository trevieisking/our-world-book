# Chapter 7 support register — pages 201–208

This register is authoritative for pages 201–208 until the large book-wide ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **201 — eukaryotic architecture:** eukaryotes share nucleus/endomembranes/cytoskeleton/mitochondrial ancestry and other major systems, but bacteria/archaea are not “primitive” or unevolved. **Status: ESTABLISHED.**
- **202 — archaeal ancestry:** strong phylogenomics supports eukaryotic emergence from within/next to Asgard archaea in a two-domain-style tree; exact sister placement remains method-sensitive. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED broad placement / exact branch ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **203 — living Asgard clues:** cultured *Prometheoarchaeum syntrophicum* demonstrates protrusions and syntrophic ecology in a close archaeal relative; living Asgards are cousins, not direct ancestors. **Status: ESTABLISHED cell biology / ancestral-state inference limited.**
- **204 — 2026 Asgard contribution:** Tobiasson et al. (2026) reports dominant Asgard association across most conserved eukaryotic functional systems, with alphaproteobacterial contributions concentrated strongly in mitochondrial functions. Sampling/HGT caveats remain. **Status: MAJOR CURRENT RESULT / not final consensus on all gene origins or event ordering.**
- **205 — mitochondrial endosymbiosis:** bacterial origin of mitochondria is established by genomes, ribosomes, membranes, division and phylogeny; exact physical uptake mechanism and ancestral metabolism remain unresolved. **Status: ESTABLISHED origin / mechanism UNCERTAIN.**
- **206 — gene transfer and organelle integration:** extensive endosymbiotic gene transfer moved mitochondrial genes to the nuclear genome; no accepted living primary amitochondrial eukaryote exists. **Status: ESTABLISHED.**
- **207 — FECA versus LECA:** FECA and LECA bracket a poorly sampled stem interval; LECA was already highly complex and mitochondria-bearing. Ordering of mitochondrial acquisition versus host cellular elaboration remains debated. **Status: ESTABLISHED endpoints / internal chronology ACTIVE FRONTIER.**
- **208 — oxygen/energetics:** eukaryogenesis does not correlate simply with a planetary oxygen threshold. Mitochondrial energetics may have enabled later complexity, but oxygen and mitochondria are not treated as single sufficient causes. **Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED caution / causal models debated.**
