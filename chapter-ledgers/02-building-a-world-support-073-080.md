# Chapter 2 support register — pages 073–080

This compact register is authoritative for pages 073–080 until the large book-wide Evidence and Image Ledgers are batch-reconciled.

## Evidence checkpoints
- **073:** cooling depended on coupled magma–atmosphere evolution. Strongly supported. Sources: Vulpius et al. 2026; Boukaré et al. 2025.
- **074:** Hadean protocrust formed early and was likely initially mafic; modern-style continents are not implied. Sources: Turner et al. 2025; Kirkland et al. 2025.
- **075:** outgassing and mantle redox controlled early atmospheric chemistry. Sources: Trail et al. 2011; Vulpius et al. 2026.
- **076:** primordial H-rich envelopes and atmospheric escape are model-supported; exact early atmosphere remains uncertain. Source: Young et al. 2023.
- **077:** Earth's water inventory is multi-stage; carbonaceous asteroidal material contributed, but no single source is adopted. Sources: Péron et al. 2021; Budde et al. 2019; Yokoyama et al. 2023.
- **078:** an initial hydrosphere by about 4.4 Ga is a supported inference, not a directly preserved ocean. Source: Vulpius et al. 2026.
- **079:** Jack Hills zircon age 4.404 ± 0.008 Ga is established; water–rock interaction is inferred from isotope chemistry. Sources: Wilde et al. 2001; Mojzsis et al. 2001; Valley et al. 2014.
- **079B:** meteoric-water interaction with crustal magmatic systems by about 4.0 Ga or earlier. Source: Johnson et al. 2024.
- **080:** direct rock evidence, zircon inference and numerical models remain explicitly separated.
