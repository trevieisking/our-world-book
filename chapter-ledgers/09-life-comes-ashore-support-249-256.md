## Scope and evidence boundary

The opening pages follow terrestrial habitats, early microbial occupation, algal relationships, plant fossils and fungi. Fossil occurrence, ecological interpretation, phylogenetic inference and molecular dates retain separate evidential strengths. Wet continental habitats do not automatically establish permanent dry-land residence.
